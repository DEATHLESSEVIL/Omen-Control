Restart required

Restart to load OmenFanBridge 0.4.0. Open OMEN Control from Applications after login. The menu-bar service starts quietly unless you previously turned it off.

Settings contains temperature units, Dock visibility and the background-service switch. Validate RPM and Auto first; remain present during initial manual fan tests.

Documentation: /Library/Application Support/OMEN Control/Documentation

Verified EFI recovery copies: /Library/Application Support/OMEN Control/Backups (administrator access).

If Installer reported an error, do not assume the EFI update succeeded. Read its log and follow the recovery instructions before rebooting.
